About me

Design is a
conversation,
not a delivery.

I came to product design through cognitive science, which means I think about behavior before I think about pixels. Good design doesn't impress — it disappears. My job is to make the right thing easy and the wrong thing hard.

I've worked embedded on product teams where I owned design end-to-end, and inside design orgs where I focused on systems and craft standards. I'm most effective when I'm close to decisions — not three handoffs away from them.

Outside work: ceramics, endurance running, reading philosophy I don't fully understand.

How I Work

Discover

Listen before drawing.

Every project starts with talking to the people who will use it. I find the gap between what users say they want and what they actually need.

Define

Agree on the problem first.

A precise problem statement saves weeks. I write it in plain language, validate it with stakeholders, and pin it to every wall and doc that follows.

Design

Explore widely, commit quickly.

I sketch fast and share early — ugly prototypes that answer real questions beat polished mockups that assume the answers.

Deliver

Ship, then watch.

Launch is a hypothesis, not a conclusion. I stay close after release, track behavior, and iterate based on what people actually do.

Let's make something worth making.
